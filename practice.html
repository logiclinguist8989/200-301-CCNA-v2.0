Find the subnet for this host

Memory trickMagic number = 256 − the interesting octet of the mask. Find the octet where the mask is not 255 or 0, list the multiples of the magic number in that octet, and the host's subnet starts at the multiple just below it. The broadcast is the next subnet minus one.
